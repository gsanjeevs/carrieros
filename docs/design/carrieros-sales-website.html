FMCSA compliance — built in, not bolted on

  • FMCSA-compliant DVIR checklist pre-loaded — no setup required

The Workflow

Load in. Invoice out.
Everything in between.

The happy path from rate confirmation to payment — without touching a spreadsheet.

01

Get the load in

Forward the email, upload the PDF, or paste the text. AI extracts every field. You review and confirm.

02

Accept & dispatch

Accept the load, assign to a truck and driver. Driver gets a push notification with all load details.

03

Share the tracking link

One tap sends your customer a live tracking link. They see status and ETA — no login needed.

04

Driver delivers & captures POD

Driver snaps the POD photo on delivery, uploads it from the app, and marks the load Delivered.

05

Invoice in 2 minutes

CarrierOS prompts you to invoice. Everything is pre-filled. Review, hit send. Done.

06

Get paid, close the load

When payment arrives, mark it paid. The load closes. Your records stay clean.
